How to use Lyric Slide Maker

1. Add your lyrics

Paste a song into the box on the left, or import a .txt or ChordPro file. Put the song title on the first line, and section labels like Verse 1, Chorus, or Bridge on their own lines — they auto-detect and color-tag.

2. Choose how slides break

By lyric lines puts a fixed number of lyric lines on each slide. By display lines packs as many lines as fit under a rendered-line cap. Use the checkboxes to keep sections from merging and to build a title slide.

3. Style it

Set aspect ratio, font, size, colors, and alignment. Changes apply to every slide live, or click Apply style to all.

4. Edit slides

Click a slide to select it. Edit text directly, change its tag, add or duplicate slides, and drag a slide by its header to reorder. Delete all clears every slide at once.

Selecting multiple slides

Shift+click selects every slide between the last one you clicked and the one you click. Ctrl/⌘+click adds or removes a single slide from the selection. Copy, cut, paste, and delete then act on the whole selection at once.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Shift / Ctrl/⌘ + click — select a range / toggle one slide
  • Ctrl/⌘ + A — select all slides
  • Ctrl/⌘ + C — copy the selected slides
  • Ctrl/⌘ + X — cut the selected slides
  • Ctrl/⌘ + V — paste after the last selected slide
  • Delete / Backspace — delete the selected slides

5. Export

Click Export .pptx to download a PowerPoint file. Your work is saved automatically in this browser.
